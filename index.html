Storefront Mastery · Pricing schedule 2026–2027

Make downtowns worth the trip.

Storefront Mastery is a downtown experience strategy practice. We work with special improvement districts, BIDs, Main Street programs, and municipalities to give people more reasons to visit a downtown, stay longer, and come back.

The work sits where the single business meets the district: storefronts, customer experience, business clusters, and the rules that govern what owners can build. We diagnose the street, find the few moves with the most leverage, and carry them toward visible change. Founded in 2019 by architect and urbanist Jaime J. Izurieta, based in Montclair, New Jersey.

This page lists what every service costs, so you can plan a budget, compare options, and bring a number to your board before we talk. Every price is fixed. Nothing is billed by the hour unless you ask for advisory time. For projects, writing, and books, visit storefrontmastery.com.

01 · How pricing works

One scope, one price, decided before we start.

Two things set the price of a corridor engagement: what you buy and how big the corridor is. We count your corridor from a map before the proposal goes out, and that class is locked when you sign.

Understand

Diagnose the street

Corridor Vital Signs reads the whole corridor. Storefront Performance Scorecards read individual businesses. Both give you a baseline you can measure against later.

Design

Find the leverage

The Idea Factory turns the diagnosis into five strategies, each with a big idea, a toolkit, an action plan, and a quick win you can show your board.

Activate

Change what people see

Storefront Accelerators take selected businesses to contractor-ready recommendations. Code work removes the rules that stand in the way.

A frontage is one ground-floor commercial unit facing the corridor, occupied or vacant. A block face is one side of one block. When the two counts land in different classes, the higher class applies. Areas larger than 220 frontages are split into two corridors, and the second is priced at 10% off.

04 · Travel, credits, and terms

What changes the number, and what does not.

Prices change only for scope, distance, schedule, or a trade agreed in writing. Every reduction below is in exchange for something specific.

Credits and upgrades

  • Alignment Workshop: full fee credited toward Tier 2 or Tier 3 within 6 months.
  • Tier 2: full fee credited toward Tier 3 within 12 months.
  • Scorecard: $400 credited toward that storefront's Accelerator within 12 months.
  • Code Friction Audit: full fee credited toward a Code Refresh within 6 months.

Ways to lower the cost

  • Two or more districts contracting together: 10% each.
  • Full payment at signing: 5%.
  • Districts with an annual budget under $300,000: up to 15% on Tier 2, Tier 3, the strategy package, or an Accelerator batch, in exchange for case-study and photo rights.
  • Combined reductions are capped at 15% and do not apply to the workshop, the audit, or the Code Refresh.

Schedule

Delivery in under three weeks from signed agreement and receipt of your data adds 15%. Standard timelines are agreed in the proposal.

Scope and exclusions

Storefront Mastery provides strategy, design direction, and implementation guidance. Legal advice, municipal adoption, enforcement, architectural drawings, construction documents, and engineered plans are outside the scope unless a proposal says otherwise.

Validity

Prices are effective November 2026 and reviewed each January. Quotes already issued are honored at their stated price.

Who pays

Engagements can be contracted by a district management corporation, a nonprofit, or a municipality. Tell us which at the start so the proposal fits your approval path.
