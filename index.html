The Corridor Diagnostic · $1,200

Two hours on your street. A report the same day.

Before anyone spends a grant dollar, redesigns a facade, or rewrites a rule, you should know what your corridor is doing to the people who walk it. The Corridor Diagnostic is a structured walk of your main street, recorded block by block in our Corridor Vital Signs field app, with the findings report delivered as soon as the walk is done.

What we measure

Six vital signs, observed on the street.

Every measure comes from what can be seen and checked on the walk, so the findings rest on evidence your board can verify for itself.

VisibilityCan people find the businesses from the sidewalk and the car?
Parking and arrivalIs it clear where to park and how to get in?
AccessibilityCan everyone reach the door and move along the block?
Street appealDo the facades and sidewalks make people want to stay?
Evening lifeDoes the street stay lit, open, and inviting after five?
Care and safetyDoes the corridor look looked-after and feel safe?

What you receive

A report you can put in front of a board the next morning.

One PDF, scorecard and findings together.

  1. The scorecard. Your corridor scored on each vital sign, with the evidence behind every score.
  2. Block by block. Each block assessed on its own, so you see where the corridor holds and where it breaks.
  3. The winning asset. What your street already has that can carry the fix.
  4. Findings and recommendations. What each vital sign tells you and what to do about it first.

  • Towns weighing a code change who need evidence from the street

Inside ASSESS

A report every business owner can act on.

Each Storefront Performance Scorecard is seven pages, generated from what is recorded on the street and reviewed before it goes out. It turns a storefront into a short list of decisions.

  1. The score. Performance out of 100 across five vital signs: upper facade, sign, windows, entrance, and lighting.
  2. What it means. What the score costs the business in visibility, invitation, upkeep, brand, and its effect on the block.
  3. Fix first. Every finding ranked, critical before watch, with the specific fix for each.

Holding a storefront or facade grant?

Assess every storefront before the first check goes out.

Without a plan, grant money goes to whatever each owner asks for first. ASSESS gives every business in your program a scored report with ranked upgrades and planning-level costs, so the money lands where it changes the street.

What it prevents

  • Everyone asks for a sign. The sign gets funded while the dark window and the dead entrance stay as they are.
  • Quotes for different jobs. Without a written scope, each contractor bids on different work.
  • The fund runs dry mid-list. The strongest projects show up late and find the pool spent.

What you do with it

  • Attach each report to its grant agreement as the approved scope
  • Check contractor quotes against the cost ranges
  • Show your board and your funder why each project was funded
  • Keep the reports as the before picture for year-end reporting

What it costs against the grant

About 4¢

per grant dollar on a $100,000 program across eight businesses. Many programs allow technical assistance costs, so check whether your grant can cover the fee.

ASSESS reports are assessments. They do not include drawings, construction documentation, renderings, or technical specifications. Permits, drawings, and installation go through your contractors and licensed professionals. Owners choose which ranked upgrades to pursue, and your committee decides what the grant covers.

Our approach

Places are made block by block.

Anyone can now generate a handsome picture of a downtown in seconds. Those images look like everywhere, which is another way of saying they look like nowhere. Your street's character lives in its real facades, its real signs, its real owners, and the rules that shaped them.

One

We read the block

Every block has its own winning asset, its own weak frontage, and its own owners. We assess and prescribe at that scale, so every recommendation fits the place it is meant for.

Two

We design for the real street

Our recommendations start from the buildings you have, the businesses inside them, and the history in their walls. The goal is a street that looks like itself, only better.

Three

We change the rules

A grant fixes one facade once. A better code improves every facade that comes after it. That is why code reform sits at the center of our implementation work.

Questions

Common questions.

Where should we start?

With the Corridor Diagnostic. In two hours on your street and a same-day report, you learn what is broken, block by block, and which fixes come first. From there, the findings point to the right next step, whether that is ALIGN, ASSESS, ACTIVATE, or implementation work.

Does the Diagnostic fee count toward the Flagship Program?

No. The Diagnostic is a standalone product with its own flat fee of $1,200, and it is not credited toward other services. ACTIVATE includes a Diagnostic of its own, walked with your downtown leaders and champions. The program tiers carry their own credits, listed under terms.

How do the program prices add up?

ALIGN is $1,950 on its own. ASSESS is $3,950 on its own. Together, as the ALIGN + ASSESS package, they are $4,950. ACTIVATE is $13,800 and includes ALIGN and ASSESS.

Move up to ACTIVATE within 6 months and what you paid for ASSESS or the package comes off in full. The package is the best route: $4,950 now and $8,850 later adds up to the same $13,800. ALIGN bought on its own is not credited.

Which program tier is right for us?

Choose ALIGN if your board, staff, and businesses do not yet agree on priorities. Choose ASSESS if you are about to fund storefront improvements or a facade program and want each business to know where the money will do the most good. Choose ACTIVATE if you want a district strategy your partners can act on.

If you are unsure, tell us what you are trying to decide and we will recommend one.

Are design services included?

Each ASSESS scorecard includes a design prescription for that storefront: one big decision, a palette, the sign text, and a window strategy. Full design direction for a storefront is the Storefront Accelerator, from $2,950 per storefront. Architectural drawings, construction documents, and permit sets are not included in any service.

Can you help rewrite our sign and storefront rules?

Yes. The Code Friction Audit shows where your current rules work against your street, and the Retail Design Code Refresh rewrites your signage, storefront, facade, and design-review provisions town-wide, with draft ordinance language for your municipal attorney. Legal review, adoption, and enforcement stay with the town.

Can we pay for ASSESS out of our grant?

Often, yes. Many programs allow administration or technical assistance costs, and some state funds budget for a paid program administrator. Check your grant agreement. We write the scope in your funder's language so it is easy to approve.

What if an owner disagrees with the ranking?

Owners choose which ranked upgrades to pursue. The ranking and the cost ranges stay as written, and your committee decides what the grant covers.

Where do the cost ranges come from?

From storefront projects we have guided and regional pricing. They frame the contractor quotes your program already asks for, so you can spot the one that is off.

Do you rescore the storefronts after the work?

No. The assessment sets the plan before the money goes out, and the reports double as the baseline you report against.

¿Está disponible en español?

Sí. Todos los servicios están disponibles en español, incluidos los reportes para los dueños de negocio.
